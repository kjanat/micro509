Migration guide ​

For a migration from the separate jose library, see Migrate from jose.

Each section covers the changes one release requires, newest first.

For the full change history, see the changelog. The certificate guide, revocation guide, and the generated API pages cover the current workflows.

0.18 to 0.19 ​

Revocation checking fails closed in more places: delegated OCSP responders and CRL signers need revocation evidence, delta CRLs are read newest first, and malformed input reports malformed.

Supply revocation evidence for delegated OCSP responders ​

responderRevocationPolicy now defaults to 'nocheck-or-evidence'. A delegated responder without id-pkix-ocsp-nocheck or noRevAvail fails with responder_revocation_unknown unless responderRevocationCrls shows it is not revoked. Supply the responder's CRLs, or ask its CA to issue responder certificates with id-pkix-ocsp-nocheck.

'require-evidence' rejects a responder carrying noRevAvail, since no CRL can list it.

'honor-nocheck' keeps the tolerant behaviour: a revoked verdict rejects, and so do a CRL entry for the responder whose reason code unrecognizedReasonCode: 'reject' refuses and an applicable delta CRL that cannot settle the responder's status. A responder with a critical cRLDistributionPoints extension still needs evidence under it. For chain revocation, set policy.ocspResponderRevocation. For checkCertificateRevocation, set ocspResponderRevocation; the CRLs in its evidence, delta CRLs included, serve as responder evidence.

With allowChainedResponderCertificate, the same policy applies to every CA certificate between the issuer and the responder. Under the default policy, supply a CRL from each such certificate's issuer.

Supply revocation evidence for delegated CRL signers ​

A delegated CRL signer outside the chain without noRevAvail or id-pkix-ocsp-nocheck now needs CRLs that cover every reason, from its issuer or from an authorized indirect CRL issuer, also when that issuer is a chain certificate or the trust anchor. Every CA on the signer's path outside the chain needs the same. Until they do, the chain reports crl_signer_indeterminate for the certificates its CRLs cover, and only a revocation in those CRLs counts. Add the issuer's CRL to crls, or issue the signer certificate with noRevAvail or id-pkix-ocsp-nocheck.

A CRL the signer signed itself proves its own status only when the signer's cRLDistributionPoints names it as cRLIssuer. A signer that shares its CA's name and signs the CA's CRLs with a separate key needs a CRL from the CA key, or policy.crlSignerSelfEvidence: 'any'.

checkChainRevocation accepts distrustedSpkiSha256, and verifyCertificateChain({ revocation }) passes its own list on. A CRL signer or delegated OCSP responder whose path carries a listed key yields no evidence.

Expect revocations from CRLs past nextUpdate ​

A CRL past its nextUpdate, older than the age bound, or without nextUpdate and an age bound still revokes an unexpired certificate it lists, unless the reason is certificateHold, or removeFromCRL in a delta CRL (RFC 5280 §5.3.1). Such a CRL now yields revoked instead of stale_crl in chain revocation, checkCertificateRevocation, checkCertificateRevocationAgainstCrl and delegated responder checks. A clean current base CRL beside such a delta CRL also yields revoked, and so does a newer clean delta CRL beside it.

A delta CRL past its nextUpdate that lists no such entry now leaves the status to its base CRL in checkCertificateRevocationAgainstCrl and checkCertificateRevocation, instead of failing with stale_crl.

Expect checkCertificateRevocation to pool delta CRLs ​

checkCertificateRevocation used to check each CRL evidence entry as its own complete CRL and delta CRL pair. It now checks each distinct complete CRL once, updated by the newest applicable delta CRL among every deltaCrl in evidence, as checkChainRevocation does. Two entries for one complete CRL, one with an older delta CRL that holds the certificate and one with a newer clean delta CRL, now yield good. A delta CRL that cannot settle the status records the new code delta_crl_unusable in details.indeterminateEvidence, and a candidate the attempt limits leave unread records delta_crl_retry_limit_exceeded there. Either one keeps OCSP or other CRL evidence from settling the status as good. Unless other evidence revokes the certificate, the result is then indeterminate with the top-level code revocation_status_indeterminate. An undecodable deltaCrl no longer hides its complete CRL, so a clean complete CRL beside it can still yield good. Evidence that does not decode records malformed in details.indeterminateEvidence.

Each CRL in evidence now counts as a delta CRL when it carries the delta CRL indicator and as a complete CRL otherwise, whichever of crl and deltaCrl holds it. The delta CRL checks of delegated OCSP responders now share the 32 checks per call, and validateOcspResponse spends at most 32 on its own.

Expect delta CRLs to be read newest first ​

Delta CRLs are now read newest first whether or not they are current, so a newer delta CRL past its nextUpdate that revokes an unexpired certificate for a reason other than certificateHold yields revoked beside older current clean ones. A delta CRL past its nextUpdate without such an entry no longer ends the search. When the attempt limits leave a delta CRL unread, the result is indeterminate with delta_crl_retry_limit_exceeded instead of good, unless the complete CRL holds a revocation no delta CRL can remove. Two delta CRLs whose thisUpdate and CRL number order them differently rank alike, and the stricter of their results stands.

The new unreadSupersededDeltaCrls: 'ignore' option of RevocationPolicy and checkCertificateRevocation, and responderRevocationUnreadSupersededDeltaCrls: 'ignore' of validateOcspResponse, skip an unread delta CRL that a delta CRL already read supersedes by both thisUpdate and CRL number: after a good status, one the delta CRL settling it supersedes, and otherwise one past its nextUpdate, without one or over the age bound. A permanent revocation that only such an older delta CRL lists then goes unread. The default 'indeterminate' counts every unread candidate, so excess delta CRLs, forged ones included, can make a status indeterminate, which mode: 'soft-fail' allows and hard-fail denies.

Pass a valid clockSkewMs ​

A negative or non-finite clockSkewMs now throws RangeError, as an invalid CRL maximum age does, in validateCertificateRevocationList, checkCertificateRevocationAgainstCrl, checkCertificateRevocation, validateOcspResponse, checkChainRevocation and verifyCertificateChain({ revocation }).

Input that does not decode now fails with malformed:

Expect a revoked CA under a bare trust anchor to be checked ​

Below a bare trust anchor, the last chain certificate is the first certificate of the path, and checkChainRevocation now checks it. Pass the anchor as trustAnchor; verifyCertificateChain passes the one its path ends at. With trustAnchor set, a chain that ends with the anchor's own certificate, or whose last certificate the anchor did not issue, throws RangeError.

0.14 to 0.15 ​

Certificate issuance, CRL creation and freshness, OCSP responder trust, and revocation result types.

Supply the issuer certificate ​

createCertificate used to accept the issuer's public key as issuerPublicKey. It now accepts the issuer certificate as issuerCertificate (PEM, DER, or parsed certificate). The builder uses the certificate's subject key identifier for the authority key identifier. If the issuer certificate has no subject key identifier, micro509 derives one from its public key.

In 0.14.0, pass the issuer public key:

ts
const leaf = await createCertificate({
  issuer: { commonName: 'Example CA' },
  subject: { commonName: 'service.example' },
  publicKey: leafKeys.publicKey,
  signerPrivateKey: ca.keyPair.privateKey,
  issuerPublicKey: ca.keyPair.publicKey,
});

In 0.15.0, pass the issuer certificate:

ts
const leaf = await createCertificate({
  issuer: { commonName: 'Example CA' },
  subject: { commonName: 'service.example' },
  publicKey: leafKeys.publicKey,
  signerPrivateKey: ca.keyPair.privateKey,
  issuerCertificate: ca.certificate.pem,
});

issuerPublicKey is no longer accepted. An issuer value that cannot be parsed throws a ResultError with code issuer_certificate_invalid. Supplying the certificate also aligns the authority key identifier with issuers whose subject key identifier was not derived by the default method.

Provide required CRL fields ​

createCertificateRevocationList now requires issuerCertificate, crlNumber, and nextUpdate. It always writes the Authority Key Identifier, CRL Number, and nextUpdate fields. nextUpdate must encode a time at least one second after thisUpdate (which defaults to the current time).

In 0.14.0, the issuer public key, CRL number, and next update were optional:

ts
const crl = await createCertificateRevocationList({
  issuer: { commonName: 'Example CA' },
  signerPrivateKey: ca.keyPair.privateKey,
});

Version 0.15.0 requires the issuer certificate, CRL number, and next update:

ts
const crl = await createCertificateRevocationList({
  issuer: { commonName: 'Example CA' },
  signerPrivateKey: ca.keyPair.privateKey,
  issuerCertificate: ca.certificate.pem,
  crlNumber: 1n,
  thisUpdate: new Date(),
  nextUpdate: new Date(Date.now() + 24 * 60 * 60 * 1000),
});

If nextUpdate does not fall in a later second after both times are truncated to whole seconds, the builder throws a ResultError with code next_update_not_after_this_update. A CRL number that is negative, non-integral, or larger than the permitted 20 encoded octets throws with code crl_number_invalid. An invalid issuer certificate throws issuer_certificate_invalid.

Handle CRL numbers as bigint ​

The builder still accepts a number or bigint for crlNumber and baseCrlNumber. Use bigint for parsed values and for numbers above Number.MAX_SAFE_INTEGER. Parsed CRL fields now have type bigint, avoiding loss of precision for valid CRL numbers up to 20 octets.

ts
const parsed = parseCertificateRevocationListDerOrThrow(crl.der);
const number: bigint | undefined = parsed.crlNumber;

if (number !== undefined && number > 100n) {
  console.log('CRL is newer than number 100');
}

Code that previously compared a parsed CRL number to a numeric literal must review the operation. Relational comparisons between bigint and number are allowed, but equality comparisons and arithmetic have different type and conversion rules. Use a bigint literal such as 100n for equality and arithmetic with a parsed CRL number. Convert to number only when the value is within the safe integer range and an API requires a number.

Handle unrecognized CRL reason codes ​

CRL reason codes outside the recognized values are no longer dropped. Parsed reason values use ParsedRevocationReason: recognized values are strings, while unknown values are represented as { type: 'unrecognized', code: bigint }. The code is bigint so values of any length are represented exactly.

ts
const reason = parsed.revokedCertificates[0]?.reasonCode;

if (reason !== undefined && typeof reason !== 'string') {
  console.log(`Unknown CRL reason: ${reason.code}`);
}

Consumers that display or switch on a reason must now handle the object variant. The unrecognizedReasonCode option on CRL checks and chain revocation policy controls how evidence with an unknown reason affects status. It defaults to 'revoked'. Choosing 'reject' returns an unrecognized-reason error and prevents other evidence from settling the status as good. A critical CRL reason with an unrecognized value makes the CRL malformed.

Handle OCSP reason codes as bigint ​

revocationReasonCode on a revoked ParsedOcspCertStatus and on the revoked result from checkCertificateRevocation is now bigint | undefined. revocationReasonFromCode also takes a bigint. Use 6n, for example, when comparing a parsed reason with certificateHold, and pass 6n to revocationReasonFromCode. The createOcspResponse builder still accepts numeric reason codes such as 6.

When serializing parsed CRL numbers or reason codes to JSON, convert those fields to decimal strings with .toString(). JSON.stringify does not serialize bigint values directly.

Bind trusted OCSP responders to their issuer ​

trustedOcspResponders on checkChainRevocation and verifyCertificateChain({ revocation }) now takes TrustedOcspResponder entries instead of bare responder certificates. Replace each responder value with an object naming both its issuing-CA scope and the responder certificate:

ts
const trustedOcspResponders = [
  {
    issuerCertificate: caCertificatePem,
    responderCertificate: responderCertificatePem,
  },
];

issuerCertificate identifies the CA whose certificates this responder may report on. If a responder is trusted for several issuers, supply an entry for each issuer. Trust for one CA no longer grants authority over every CA in the validated path.

Set an age policy for CRLs without nextUpdate ​

A received complete CRL without nextUpdate now fails with stale_crl unless you supply a maximum age for its thisUpdate. Choose the age according to your revocation policy, using the option for the API you call:

validateCertificateRevocationList, checkCertificateRevocationAgainstCrlmaxAgeMs

For example, 24 * 60 * 60 * 1000 bounds the age to one day. An expired nextUpdate still fails even when an age bound is supplied. Delta CRLs must have nextUpdate.

The chain-level age policy also applies to CRL signers and delegated OCSP responders. Under the default hard-fail policy, a CRL without nextUpdate and without an age bound cannot establish good status. Other applicable evidence may still establish the certificate's status.

Handle skipped revocation results ​

Chain revocation results now include status: 'skipped' for certificates whose signed extensions exempt them from revocation checks. The skipReason is 'no_rev_avail' for noRevAvail, or 'ocsp_nocheck' for id-pkix-ocsp-nocheck.

Add this case to exhaustive switches over result.value.certificates from checkChainRevocation. Display the exemption reason separately from a validated good verdict. A skipped entry has no source or revocationInfo, and no revocation evidence was consulted for that certificate.

checkCertificateRevocation returns status: 'skipped' with skipReason: 'no_rev_avail' for a certificate carrying noRevAvail. A CRL never lists such a certificate (RFC 9608 §2), so a CRL without it shows nothing. A certificate carrying id-pkix-ocsp-nocheck is still checked against the evidence.

Update exhaustive type handling ​

SubjectAltName has additional typed variants, including upn, krb5PrincipalName and smtpUtf8Mailbox. Code that previously handled these values through generic otherName objects must handle their typed variants. Review exhaustive switches over GeneralName values and error-code unions when compiling against 0.15.0.
